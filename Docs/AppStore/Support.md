# Spectrogram support

Spectrogram displays a live spectrogram from your microphone on iPhone and iPad.

- Allow microphone access when prompted. If you previously denied access, enable Microphone for Spectrogram in Settings.
- Tap pause to freeze the spectrogram; tap play to resume.
- Tap a horizontal slice to inspect its frequency spectrum. Tap or drag along the plot to select a nearby peak and see its frequency and nearest musical note.
- Use the sliders button to adjust the frequency range, display noise floor, and contrast.
- Tap the trash button to clear the displayed history.

Musical notes refer to individual frequency peaks, which can be harmonics rather than the fundamental pitch. Microphone hardware and environmental noise affect the measurement.

Audio is processed on your device and is not recorded or uploaded. See the [privacy policy](Privacy.md).

To report a problem or request a feature, [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/dllu/spectrogram-ios/issues). Include your device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Issues are public; please leave out private information.
