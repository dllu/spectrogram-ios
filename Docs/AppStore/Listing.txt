Name: Simple Spectrogram
Subtitle: Live audio spectrum & notes
Category: Utilities
Version: 1.0
Price: Free

Description:
See the frequencies in the sound around you. Spectrogram turns your microphone input into a smooth, real-time spectrogram with a clear logarithmic frequency scale and Inferno colors.

• Watch sound evolve over time, with the newest audio at the top.
• Pause and tap any slice to inspect its frequency spectrum.
• Tap or drag on the spectrum to find nearby peaks and their exact frequencies.
• See the nearest musical note and the difference in hertz and cents, referenced to A4 = 440 Hz.
• Adjust the frequency range, noise floor, and contrast.
• Use portrait or landscape on iPhone and iPad.

Audio is analyzed on your device in memory. It is never recorded, uploaded, or shared. No account, ads, or tracking.

Musical note labels describe individual spectral peaks, which may be harmonics rather than a sound’s fundamental frequency.

Keywords:

Review notes:
No sign-in is required. Allow microphone access to display the live spectrogram. Tap pause to freeze it; tap a horizontal slice to open its spectrum, then tap or drag on the plot to select a peak. Display settings are available through the sliders button. All audio processing is on-device; audio is not saved or transmitted.
