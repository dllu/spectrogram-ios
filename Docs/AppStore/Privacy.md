# Spectrogram privacy policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Spectrogram analyzes microphone input on your iPhone or iPad to display sound frequencies. Audio is processed in memory on your device. The app does not record audio files, transmit audio, or send your data to the developer or third parties.

Spectrogram does not collect personal data, use analytics or advertising services, track you, or require an account. Your display preferences are saved locally on your device. Spectrogram requests microphone permission only to provide the live spectrogram; you can change this permission in Settings.

If you contact the developer through GitHub, that interaction is handled by GitHub under its own privacy policy. Please do not include private audio or other sensitive information in a public issue.

For questions about Spectrogram or this policy, open an issue at https://github.com/dllu/spectrogram-ios/issues.
